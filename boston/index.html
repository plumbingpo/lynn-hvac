How Boston Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Most mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

The age of your equipment plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of the year.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is.
